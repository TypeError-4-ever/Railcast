RAILCAST
Network-aware arrival forecasting for Indian Railways
Today a passenger gets one number with nothing attached to it. RAILCAST gives an arrival window whose coverage is audited on held-out data — per zone and per horizon, not asserted once and pooled.
60% lower ETA error than carry-forward
80% arrival window, coverage audited
40h forecast horizon, station by station
Forecasting how delay will move through the network — not repeating the delay we already have.
The window narrows as the train runs. Every remaining station gets an interval. It widens with distance and closes as each actual arrival comes in — 62 minutes down to 21 on this run.
