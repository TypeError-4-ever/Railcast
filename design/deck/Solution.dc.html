PROBLEM AT HAND
Delay is repeated, not forecast Today's ETA carries the current delay forward and trusts timetable padding. It cannot see congestion building ahead, or the slack further on that will absorb it.
The network goes blind between stations Position is reported only at instrumented stations, leaving gaps of 40–60 minutes. Signal halts and crawls in between are never recorded.
Cascades are invisible One train held at a junction silently delays every train behind it, yet no deployed ETA model represents that dependency.
One number hides the risk A single time with no confidence attached, so nobody knows when it is safe to act on it — and nothing handles a cancellation or diversion mid-run.
HOW ONE FORECAST IS MADE
INNOVATION AND UNIQUENESS
Forecasts the cause, not the clock Trains are advanced over the real block-section graph until they conflict, so the ETA moves for a reason we can name and show on screen.
Says how sure it is, and proves it Mondrian conformal calibration gives an 80% window whose coverage is checked per horizon, segment and time of day — not pooled once and asserted.
Turns the window into a decision Connection-make probability, a leave-home prompt, and the cost in network-minutes of letting one train go first.
Survives a disrupted network Cancellations, diversions and short-terminations re-plan the remaining section sequence instead of breaking the forecast.
Reaches every passenger App and station displays for smartphones; SMS and IVR in regional languages for passengers who do not have one.
