TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY
•Every component is mature. LightGBM and conformal prediction are established open source with published benchmarks.
•No new trackside hardware. Consumes position feeds IR already produces, through a swappable adapter.
•Cheap at national scale. Boosted trees serve a full re-forecast on one commodity node; no GPU at serving time.
•The simulator is its own fallback. With the learned layer off, rule-based conflict resolution still beats carry-forward beyond three hours.
•Phone sensing is optional. An accuracy bonus, never a dependency. The forecast is complete without it.
HOW WE PROVE IT
1Baseline stated. Current delay carried forward, computed on exactly the same rows.
2Temporal holdout. Train on earlier months, test on later ones. A random split leaks and flatters every number.
3Error by horizon. Next station, 1–3 h, 3–8 h, 8–24 h, beyond. One pooled figure hides where a model fails.
4Coverage audited. Against the nominal 80%, broken out by zone, horizon and time of day.
5Shadow mode. Run live beside the existing ETA on one corridor before a passenger is shown anything.
RISKS AND HOW WE RETIRE THEM
RISK RETIRED BY
No live IR feed in development FeedAdapter plus a movement simulator; swap the adapter at deployment.
Error compounds over a 40-hour run Direct multi-horizon residual: each station forecast in one shot, never chained.
Coverage holds on average, fails in the monsoon Mondrian calibration by zone, horizon and season, with coverage alarmed rather than assumed.
Controllers distrust a black box Per-section attribution, the blocking train named. Advisory only — never an automatic movement order.
Phone motion is not train motion Low-pass filter, gyro gating, multi-handset consensus; degrades to the timetable model.
