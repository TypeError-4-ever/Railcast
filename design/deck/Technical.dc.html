WHAT MAKES THE FORECAST MOVE
Arrivals forced monotone A run can never arrive somewhere before it arrives at the station behind it.
Attribution on every change Names the blocking train, the section and the feature responsible.
Online correction at every halt Each actual arrival is a free label: the rest of the run re-forecasts.
Drift monitor and retraining Weekly incremental refit, alarm on coverage or MAE drift by zone.
